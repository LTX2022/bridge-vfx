# Causal diagnosis

Normalize feedback into a subject/component, ROI, frame range, perceptual dimension, direction, severity, protected dimensions, and uncertainty. Shared dimensions include shape, scale, distribution, spatial detail, motion, timing, energy, interaction, material/optics, lighting, render quality, and composite integration.

Do not map a phrase directly to a parameter. Locate the earliest stage where the expected evidence is lost. Compare, when available, raw simulation/procedural evidence, shaped geometry or fields, diagnostic shading/AOVs, pre-composite beauty, and final composite.

Each `CauseHypothesis` must name the component, stage, cause family, supporting and conflicting evidence, confidence, required recomputation, and a falsifying probe. Generate candidates from one cause family with a fixed scene, seed, camera, and evaluation frame set. Limit each issue to three candidates and each candidate to five public semantic controls.

Verification requires the target issue to improve, protected dimensions not to regress, graph editability and lineage to remain valid, and the artist to confirm the result. Metrics support that judgment but cannot replace it.
