# Vellum soft body and grains

Observe deformation, volume retention, constraint stability, damping, contact, particle packing, clumping, settling, and temporal jitter. Diagnose constraints and solve scale before material appearance. Cache only pack-declared geometry, points, and constraint artifacts.

For tearing or tutorial reconstruction, use [tutorial reconstruction](../tutorial-reconstruction.md). Validate geometry and constraint streams, rest/deformed topology, fracture membership and the simulation-to-render transfer independently. Confirm event timing and actual output provenance before tuning force. Inspect a sequence containing pre-event, event and aftermath; a separated low-resolution cage does not prove the final surface tears correctly. Geometry cleanup thresholds and stiffness values must be justified for the observed scale and input, not copied from a previous failed project.
